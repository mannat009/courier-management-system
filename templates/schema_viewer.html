Courier Entity-Relationship (ER) Architecture

3NF Normalized
1. branches Parent

Hubs, service centres and pickup points.

PK: branch_id
2. employees & 3. vehicles 1:N with Branch

Staff roles, shifts, salary and the delivery fleet.

FK: branch_id (SET NULL)
4. customers & 5. service_types Masters

Senders with reward points; pricing plans by weight.

PK: customer_id, service_id
6. shipments Master Booking

Linked to sender, 2 branches, service, agent and vehicle.

6 FKs (service_id RESTRICT)
7. tracking_events (M:N) Junction Table

Resolves the M:N between shipments & branches: a shipment passes through many branches, and a branch scans many shipments. Stores status, remarks and timestamp for each scan.

FK: shipment_id (CASCADE), branch_id (SET NULL)
Integrity Rules Enforced: PRAGMA foreign_keys = ON;, positive weight & charge bounds, status CHECK constraints, UNIQUE tracking numbers, and automatic status-sync trigger on every tracking scan.
